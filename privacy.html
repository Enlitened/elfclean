Privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026

The short version

ElfClean does not collect any data. There are no accounts, analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry, and ElfClean operates no servers.

What ElfClean reads

To measure storage, ElfClean reads file metadata (names, sizes, dates) in the folders you grant. To identify a cleanup finding it may read small metadata files from supported tools, for example a simulator's device description, or a git repository's own files to check for uncommitted or unpushed work. When you search for duplicates, ElfClean reads the files it compares to see whether their contents match; this happens only on your Mac. The Apps screen also reads the name, version and identifier of apps in /Applications to list them; removing one needs your separate grant. Your file contents and conversation transcripts are never uploaded.

What stays on your Mac

Settings, folder access grants, compact storage summaries (kept for up to 90 days) and the cleanup and restore history are stored locally in ElfClean's app container. You can clear storage summaries separately from history. If you export history as CSV, it contains local paths; review it before sharing.

Purchases

Apple's StoreKit handles product information, purchases, restoring and refunds under your Apple Account terms. ElfClean never receives your payment details.

Optional macOS features

Notifications and launch at login are optional and controlled by macOS. They never enable background deletion or send file information anywhere.

If you use the ElfClean shortcuts in Shortcuts or Siri, "Get Reclaimable Space" returns the last scan's total and your disk's free space to that shortcut, on your Mac. ElfClean sends nothing anywhere.
