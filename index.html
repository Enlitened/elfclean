It won't delete work you haven't pushed

When an AI wrote most of the code, it's easy to lose track of what's saved. ElfClean reads each project's Git repository on your Mac and shows whether it has changes that aren't committed or pushed anywhere.

Work that exists only on your Mac is marked At risk and never ticked for you, and it's checked again just before anything is removed.

Review everything. Get anything back.

  • Nothing moves until you've seen what it is, what removing it changes and how to get it back.
  • Items go to the Trash, and History remembers where each came from so it can put it back while it's still there.
  • Running apps, Apple and system apps, credentials, cloud drives and anything you mark Never Touch are left alone.
  • Honest numbers: measured sizes, no health score, no claim that cleaning speeds up your Mac.

Questions

Will ElfClean delete anything without asking?
No. Scanning only reads. Nothing moves until you've reviewed it and confirmed. Items go to the Trash by default, and History can put them back while they're still there. Space is freed when you empty the Trash.
Is it safe to delete node_modules on a Mac?
Usually: npm, pnpm, Yarn or Bun install it again from your lockfile. ElfClean offers node_modules only in projects you haven't touched for a while, leaves recent ones unticked, and marks projects with uncommitted or unpushed work as At risk. More on node_modules
Which AI tools does it know about?
Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Devin (formerly Windsurf), VS Code, Roo Code, Aider and Continue, plus Ollama and Hugging Face models and the Git worktrees agents leave behind. AI apps' caches and logs are covered by the app caches rules. What coding agents leave behind
Does it speed up my Mac?
No. It frees disk space and tells you exactly how much. There's no health score and no promise that cleaning makes your Mac faster.
Does it need Full Disk Access?
No. ElfClean is sandboxed. You grant it your home folder, and any other folders or disks you want measured. It measures those, not the whole disk.
Is it a subscription?
No. Scanning is free, and one purchase unlocks cleaning several items at once. There's nothing to renew.
