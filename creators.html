For creators

If you make videos, posts or newsletters about vibe coding, AI tools, local models or Mac apps, ElfClean probably has something to show your audience: how much space all of that really takes. Here's what you get and how to ask.

What you get

  • A free full unlock for your own Mac, as a one-time App Store code.
  • A code for your audience, a discount only your viewers or readers can use.
  • Your own App Store link, so you can see how many people it brought.
  • The press kit: icon, screenshots and descriptions you can use as they are.

There's no script, no approval of your video and no request for a positive review. An honest look is all that's asked. Please tell your audience you were given a free code, as your country's advertising rules require.

Ideas that film well

  • Scan before and after a week of vibe coding: how much did the AI leave behind?
  • Your local models, counted honestly: which ones share files, and what removing one really frees.
  • The At risk badge on a project you forgot to push, before anything is removed.
  • Moving a folder to the Trash, then putting it back from History.

Film against a test folder or a Mac you're comfortable showing: scans list real file names and paths.

How to ask

Email csergay@gmail.com with a link to your channel, blog or newsletter. Codes go out as soon as ElfClean is on the Mac App Store; until then, you can still get in touch and be first in line.

ElfClean is made by one developer, so you'll hear back from the person who built it.
